# Third-party credits

## Clearwater — Aurélien / Lumaris (MIT) — https://github.com/Aureliengmz/clearwater
Used: water shading model (Fresnel at IOR 1.3335, refraction onto the bed, Beer–Lambert absorption + in-scatter
coefficients, Beckmann glints, suspended specks) adapted into src/r_river.js; pebble texture (downsampled to 512 px).

## three-stylized — Steve245270533 (MIT) — https://github.com/Steve245270533/three-stylized
(itself adapted from cortiz2894/stylized-components)
Used: instanced blade-grass technique (tapered blades, coherent world wind, root-to-tip gradient, up-flattened
diffuse, back-light transmission, shadow receiving) adapted to three r128 in src/r_grass.js.
